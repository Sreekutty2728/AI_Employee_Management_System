WORK FROM HOME POLICY

1. Eligibility

Employees may be permitted to work from home based on their job role,
work requirements, and company approval.

Work from home is not automatically available to all employees.

2. Work From Home Request

Employees who want to work from home must submit a request through
the Employee Management System or follow the company's approved
request process.

The employee should provide:

- Requested date
- Reason for working from home
- Any relevant information required by the administrator

3. Approval

All work from home requests are subject to administrative approval.

The administrator may Approve or Reject the request based on:

- Business requirements
- Employee role
- Work responsibilities
- Team requirements

Employees should receive approval before working from home.

4. Working Hours

Employees working from home must follow their normal working hours
unless a different schedule has been approved.

Employees are expected to remain available during working hours.

5. Attendance

Employees working from home must record their attendance according
to the company's attendance procedure.

Employees must record their check-in and check-out when required.

6. Work Responsibilities

Employees working from home are responsible for completing their
assigned work and meeting deadlines.

Employees should remain reachable through approved communication
channels during working hours.

7. Internet and Equipment

Employees working from home should have a reliable internet
connection and the necessary equipment to perform their work.

Company equipment must be used responsibly and securely.

8. Data Security

Employees must protect company information while working from home.

Employees should not share company documents, passwords, or
confidential information with unauthorized people.

Employees should use approved company systems and applications
when accessing company information.

9. Changes or Cancellation

The administrator may cancel or modify an approved work from home
arrangement when business requirements require it.

Employees should inform the administrator if they are unable to
work from home as approved.

10. General Work From Home Rules

Work from home is provided based on company requirements and
administrative approval.

Employees must follow attendance, security, confidentiality, and
work-performance requirements while working from home.

IMPORTANT INSTRUCTION FOR AI HR ASSISTANT

The AI HR Assistant should answer work-from-home questions using
the information in this document.

If the requested information is not available in this policy,
the AI should tell the employee that the information is not
