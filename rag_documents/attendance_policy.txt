EMPLOYEE ATTENDANCE POLICY

1. Attendance Recording

Employees must record their attendance through the Employee Management System.

Attendance is recorded using:
- Check-in time
- Check-out time
- Attendance status

The system can record the following attendance statuses:
- Present
- Absent
- Leave
- Half Day

2. Check-In and Check-Out

Employees should record their check-in when they start work.

Employees should record their check-out when they finish work.

Employees should ensure that their attendance records are accurate.

3. Present

An employee is marked as Present when they attend work as scheduled and have a valid attendance record.

4. Absent

An employee may be marked as Absent when they do not attend work and do not have an approved leave request.

5. Leave

An employee may be marked as Leave when they have an approved leave request for that day.

6. Half Day

An employee may be marked as Half Day when they work only part of the scheduled working day.

7. Attendance Records

Employees can view their attendance information through the Employee Management System.

Attendance records may include:
- Date
- Check-in time
- Check-out time
- Status
- Remarks

8. Attendance Corrections

If an employee notices an incorrect attendance record, they should report the issue to the administrator or HR.

Employees should provide the date and details of the attendance issue.

9. Attendance and Leave

Approved leave and attendance records should be consistent.

Employees should not be marked Present for a day on which they have approved leave.

10. General Attendance Rules

Employees are responsible for maintaining accurate attendance records.

Employees should report attendance-related problems as soon as possible.

The administrator is responsible for reviewing and managing attendance records.
